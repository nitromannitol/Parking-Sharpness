## Method per node

1. **Statement integrity.** The cited `parking.tex` lines were extracted from the pinned
   paper and read against the bytes between the `FROZEN-STATEMENT-BEGIN`/`END` markers:
   hypotheses, quantifier order, domains, exponents and constants were compared by hand,
   with `check_exponents.py`, `check_constants.py` and `check_clauses.py` consulted as a
   cross-check.  Every existential constant the paper says depends only on `d` is bound
   before `n`, `t`, `r`, `p`, `δ`, `L`, etc.  `tools/check_manifest.py` re-checks the frozen
   bytes hash and the declared name, so no statement byte has drifted.
2. **Proof.** `lake build Parking` completed (10371 jobs) and the targeted `#print axioms`
   probe reports `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]` for every Batch B export; the
   repo-wide `check_axioms.py` reports 62 clean, 0 on `sorryAx`, 0 unresolved.  No `axiom`,
   `admit`, or other non-standard axiom occurs in the production tree.
3. **Non-vacuity / junk.** Each hypothesis block was checked for satisfiability and each
   conclusion for a junk value (`0`, `sInf ∅`, a non-summable `tsum`, `Set.ncard` on an
   infinite set, `True`).  Batch B uses `Integrable`/`Summable` guards around every integral
   and `tsum`, `iSup` of nonnegative terms, `ℕ∞` limits (`Ulimit`, `meanuLimit`) with `⊤` for
   divergence, and positive existential constants.  No junk value was found.
4. **Citations.** Every `Parking.External.*` hypothesis of a Batch B node was traced to a
   genuine cited input: Bernstein (Pinelis), Donsker-Varadhan, Green gradient (Lawler-Limic),
   Sandpile growth (Bou-Rabee-Panagiotis `thm:BP`), Stopping (BPRS Theorem 3.2), oriented
   stopping stability, spatial odometer scaling, heat interior regularity, heat compactness
   and multivariate Berry-Esseen (Raic) -- never a step the paper proves in the cited range.
   The proved `External` nodes (`uConcentration`, `greenNorms`, `heatStrongMinimum`,
   `criticalScaleLowerTail`, `varianceScale`, `binomialLocalCLT`) are discharged internally
   and correctly do not appear as hypotheses.

## CONCERNs (documented, not defects; no action requested)

1. **`ext-green-gradient` keeps only the first half of the paper display** (Batch A).  The
   paper's `eq:green-gradient` asserts the gradient bound *and, hence,* the `Γ` bound; the
   frozen Prop transcribes only the first.  The sole dependent node, `lem-gamma-sum`, carries
   the gradient bound as its hypothesis and derives the `Γ` bound, so nothing is lost.
2. **Two `definition` nodes are SEALED by a companion theorem** (Batch A).  `ext-stopping`
   and `ext-green-gradient` export a `Prop`; a targeted probe confirms their companion proofs
   (`Parking.External.stopping`, `Parking.External.greenGradient`) have closure exactly the
   three standard axioms.
3. **`lem-transport` carries a conservative extra hypothesis** (Batch B).  The paper's
   statement assumes only i.i.d. integer-valued coordinates; the frozen statement adds
   `hint : Integrable (fun k => |k|) ν`.  This is a weakening (extra hypothesis), exactly the
   condition under which the real-valued identities and the `tsum` expansion are meaningful,
   and it is flagged by `tools/check_clauses.py`.  It does not falsify or strengthen any
   consequence.
